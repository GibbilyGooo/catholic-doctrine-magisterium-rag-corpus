# Copyright and sources

## Source provenance

Every installed document is bound to a provenance entry in:

- `source/provenance/source_catalog.json`
- `source/provenance/source_editions.json`
- `source/provenance/source_hashes.json`
- `source/provenance/fragments/`

These records identify the document, authority, edition or translation, source URL, acquisition date, source locator, preserved hashes, alternate verification sources, language coverage, and known limitations.

The corpus draws principally from official Holy See publications and identified historical or authorized mirrors. It includes no copied website layout, navigation, advertising, analytics, or application code.

## Rights boundary

AD IPSUM does not claim ownership of the teaching documents of the Catholic Church or third-party translations and does not relicense those primary texts.

The presence of a source text in this research and retrieval corpus does not erase the rights statement of its publisher, host, editor, or translator. Users who redistribute, republish, or commercially exploit primary-text content should review the provenance entry and the applicable source terms for that document.

## Citation and textual responsibility

When quoting or displaying retrieved text:

1. Preserve the canonical locus.
2. Identify the document and issuing authority.
3. Preserve the original source URL when practical.
4. Do not present a paraphrase as a direct quotation.
5. Do not silently repair a documented limitation or disputed translation.
6. Verify current law, discipline, and newer documents against a current official source.

Corrections should be supported by an official source, an authorized edition, or an identifiable textual witness. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
